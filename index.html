Our story

We wired Tampa before the streetcars stopped running.

Gaylord/Miller Electric Corp has been keeping Tampa Bay powered for over a century — through booms, hurricanes, and every code book since. That kind of history isn’t marketing. It’s accountability: we’ll still be here when the work needs standing behind.

1915

Founded in Tampa — wiring homes and businesses in the streetcar and cigar-factory era.

Powering Tampa’s postwar boom: new suburbs, new schools, and air conditioning everywhere.

1980s–2000s
